## 1. Company & GCC Background

Bristol Myers Squibb is a Fortune 500 biopharmaceutical leader headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, known for blockbuster therapeutics in oncology, hematology, immunology, and cardiovascular diseases (Opdivo, Eliquis, Revlimid).

In Hyderabad, BMS operates a state-of-the-art **Global Capability Center (GCC)** located in the HITEC City / Knowledge City tech corridor. The Hyderabad center drives enterprise digital transformation, clinical data science, global supply chain analytics, and regulatory transparency reporting.

---

## 2. Team Mission: Global Transparency Data Operations

- **The Mandate:** Global pharmaceutical regulations (such as the US Sunshine Act, EFPIA in Europe, and regional global codes) mandate that pharmaceutical manufacturers report every Transfer of Value (ToV) made to **Healthcare Professionals (HCPs)** and **Healthcare Organizations (HCOs)** — including advisory board fees, research grants, meals, travel, and consulting stipends.
- **The Technical Challenge:** These transactions originate in dozens of disparate systems (Concur, Veeva CRM, SAP ERP, clinical trial portals, direct vendor feeds). The Transparency Data Operations team ingests, cleanses, reconciles, and aggregates these multi-source feeds into central audit-ready compliance repositories (e.g., MediSpend) and Databricks analytical marts.
- **Core Platform Stack:** Azure/AWS Cloud, Databricks, PySpark, SQL, Apache Airflow, Informatica MDM, and Power BI.
