# Executive Company Brief: PwC Acceleration Center India

## 1. Enterprise Profile & Acceleration Center Model
- **Organization:** PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) Global Network
- **Delivery Arm:** PwC Acceleration Centers (ACs) in India (Bengaluru, Kolkata, Hyderabad) provide advanced technological, digital, and advisory execution for PwC US and global member firms.
- **Practice:** Financial Services (FS) Advisory & Data Platform Transformation.

## 2. Technology Platform Context
- Developing modern Lakehouse architectures on Azure Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, and Snowflake for tier-1 global banking, insurance, and asset management clients.
- Heavy focus on regulatory data governance, financial reporting data lineage, and Unity Catalog access controls.
