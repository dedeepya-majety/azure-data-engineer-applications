The opportunity
We are currently seeking a seasoned Azure Data Engineer with proven experience in Databricks PySpark to join our team of professionals. The successful candidate will play a key role in our Metadata Management team, providing cutting-edge data management strategies and leveraging their strong analytical skills to solve complex problems.
Key Responsibilities:
Participate actively in a team of Engineers and Architects, leading the development of prototypes, conducting proofs of concept, and providing recommendations through the design, development, and/or operation of complex technological environments.
Collaborate closely with both business and technical resources, meeting requirements with concrete designs and implementing these designs effectively.
Conduct regular training sessions for our business and technology partners, focusing on enterprise data quality standards, guidelines, and processes.
Skills and Qualifications Needed:
Proven experience in managing and delivering large-scale data warehousing projects, preferably involving Azure Synapse or Databricks platform.
Strong SQL and Python(PySpark) skill is must for the role
Ability to handle metadata extraction and data integration across a range of resources.
A degree in computer science or a similar field, with between 3-7 years of practical experience.
At least 1-3 years of hands-on experience as a developer/designer, specifically with Databricks PySpark and Azure Synapse.
Strong ability to solve complex problems at scale.
Experience in integrating systems using ETL or APIs and designing human task workflows.
Flexible, self-motivated approach with strong commitment to problem resolution.
Excellent written and oral communication skills, with the ability to deliver complex information in a clear and effective manner to a range of different audiences.
Willingness to work globally and across different cultures, and to participate in all stages of the data solution delivery lifecycle, including pre-studies, design, development, testing, deployment, and support.
Data modelling or NoSQL experience will be an added advantage.
