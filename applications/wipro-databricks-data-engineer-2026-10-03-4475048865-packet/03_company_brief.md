## 1. Enterprise Profile & Cloud/Data Engineering Footprint
Wipro is a $11B+ global IT consultancy with over 230,000 employees globally. Its Data, Analytics & AI practice is a premier global partner with Microsoft Azure and Databricks.
- **Databricks Partnership:** Wipro holds an Elite Global Consulting Partner tier with Databricks, delivering enterprise Lakehouse migrations, generative AI data foundations, and cloud analytics platforms across life sciences, banking, manufacturing, and retail clients.
- **Client Deployment Models:** Projects range from managed service engagements to dedicated client team extensions (both GCC client support and offshore delivery centers).

---

## 2. Hiring Culture & Notice Period Urgency
- **Immediate Joiner Premium:** Indian service firms (Wipro, TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, LTIMindtree) experience severe client billing lag when hiring candidates on standard 90-day notice periods. Candidates with **30 days or fewer on their notice period** are routed directly to technical interview panels within 24-48 hours.
- **Recruitment Process:** 
  1. Technical Phone / Screening: Verifying notice period, current CTC, and Databricks project work.
  2. Technical Assessment: 1-hour virtual interview on Microsoft Teams covering PySpark, Delta Lake internals (ACID, Z-Ordering, partitioning), Spark execution engine, and SQL.
  3. Managerial / HR Discussion: Client account alignment, location flexibility (Hyderabad / Chennai hybrid), and compensation offer release.
