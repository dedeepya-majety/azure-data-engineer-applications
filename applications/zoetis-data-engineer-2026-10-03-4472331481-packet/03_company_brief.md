# Company Intelligence Brief: Zoetis, Inc.

> **Entity:** Zoetis, Inc. (NYSE: ZTS)  
> **Global Animal Health Leader (Fortune 500)**  
> **India Entity:** Zoetis India Capability Center (ZICC), Hyderabad, Telangana  
> **Global Headquarters:** Parsippany, New Jersey, USA  

---

## 1. Enterprise Profile & Hyderabad GCC Operations
Zoetis is the global market leader in animal pharmaceuticals, diagnostics, and genetic technologies for pets and livestock, with over $8.5B in annual revenue.
- **Zoetis India Capability Center (ZICC):** Located in Hyderabad, ZICC serves as Zoetis's premier global technology and digital engineering hub. The ZTD (Zoetis Tech & Digital) group delivers cloud data platforms, ERP integration, and commercial analytics for Zoetis's global supply chain and sales divisions.
- **Technology Strategy:** Migrating legacy enterprise data repositories to cloud Lakehouse architectures on Microsoft Azure and Databricks, integrating with SAP ERP, Salesforce CRM, and master data management platforms.

---

## 2. Interview & Hiring Dynamics
- **Remote Interview Architecture:** 100% virtual interview rounds conducted over Microsoft Teams.
  - Round 1: Recruiter Screen (30 mins — CTC alignment, notice period verification, motivation).
  - Round 2: Technical Deep Dive (60 mins — PySpark coding, ADF pipeline design, SQL profiling, Delta Lake features).
  - Round 3: Engineering Manager & Architecture Panel (60 mins — System design, MDM integration, GxP awareness, scenario-based problem solving).
- **Offer Dynamics:** Zoetis Hyderabad offers top-tier GCC compensation packages (typically 20%–35% above Indian IT service company offerings for the same YOE), with generous benefits and hybrid working models.
