# DEDEEPYA RECRUITER CALLS PLAYBOOK: 5-Minute Phone Screen Script

> **Keep this document open on your laptop screen during phone screens and recruiter introductory calls.**  
> Tailored specifically for conversations with corporate talent acquisition and agency recruiters for Azure Data Engineering roles.  
> Target: Project technical authority, highlight enterprise lakehouse modernization, anchor compensation, and secure written job requisitions.

---

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute 5-Minute Call Flow

### Minute 0:00 to 1:00: Warm Greeting & Small Talk
**Goal:** Sound confident, articulate, and professional. Establish an immediate collaborative rhythm.

* **Recruiter:** "Hi Dedeepya, thanks for taking the time to speak today! How are you?"
* **Dedeepya:**  
  "Hi [Recruiter Name]! I'm doing great, thank you. Thank you for connecting on LinkedIn. How is your week going?"

* **Recruiter:** "Doing well! I came across your profile and was impressed by your Databricks and Azure experience. I'd love to learn more about your background and share details about our data engineering opening."
* **Dedeepya:**  
  "Sounds wonderful! I'd be glad to give you a concise overview of my cloud data engineering work at Cognizant, and then I'd love to hear more about your team's architecture and the key priorities for this role."

---

### Minute 1:00 to 2:00: The 60-Second Core Career Pitch (Cognizant Lakehouse Modernization)
**Goal:** Deliver her exact technical craft and business impact with zero hesitation.

* **Recruiter:** "Can you walk me through your background and experience with Azure, Databricks, and PySpark?"
* **Dedeepya:**  
  "Certainly! I have 3+ years of enterprise data engineering experience at Cognizant Technology Solutions, specializing in building and scaling cloud data lakehouses on **Azure Databricks**, **Delta Lake**, and **PySpark**.

  Most recently, I spearheaded the architectural modernization of our client's legacy on-premises **Informatica PowerCenter** ETL workflows into an **Azure Databricks Delta Lake Medallion Architecture**.

  By replacing batch extraction with **Databricks Auto Loader** for continuous ingestion from ADLS Gen2, implementing PySpark window function deduplication in our Silver layer, and optimizing Delta Lake ACID merges, we achieved a **40% reduction in average pipeline runtime** and delivered **~30% cloud infrastructure cost savings** through cluster rightsizing and auto-scaling, all while maintaining **100% data parity**.

  My core daily stack is **PySpark, Azure Databricks, Delta Lake, Azure Data Factory (ADF), Apache Airflow, Unity Catalog**, and **Power BI**."

---

### Minute 2:00 to 3:00: Technical Authority & Governance (The Competitive Edge)
**Goal:** Prove that she understands enterprise governance and data quality, not just pipeline coding.

* **Recruiter:** "How do you handle data governance, pipeline orchestration, and data quality across your pipelines?"
* **Dedeepya:**  
  "We treat governance as a first-class architectural requirement. 

  On the lakehouse layer, I configured **Databricks Unity Catalog** to enforce centralized data governance, metadata search, and table-level and column-level RBAC for data compliance.

  For workflow orchestration, I build modular Python **Apache Airflow DAGs** and **Azure Data Factory** pipelines with automated retry logic, branch failure alerts, and git CI/CD integration.

  Additionally, I bring experience in **Informatica MDM**, configuring match-and-merge rule sets and trust scores to eliminate duplicate customer entities and build single golden master records. I also hold both the **Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate** and **Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate** certifications."

---

### Minute 3:00 to 4:00: Logistics, Location & Compensation
**Goal:** Set firm, professional compensation anchors and establish availability.

* **Recruiter:** "What are your salary expectations, and are you open to relocation or hybrid work?"
* **Dedeepya:**  
  "In terms of location, I am based in Chennai, and I am completely open to hybrid or remote arrangements, as well as relocation to major hubs such as Bangalore, Hyderabad, or international opportunities.

  Regarding compensation, based on my 3+ years of production experience modernizing enterprise ETL to Azure Databricks and my official Databricks and Microsoft certifications:
  - For India-based roles, I am targeting **₹16 LPA to ₹22 LPA** depending on the location and hybrid structure.
  - For international / US-facing remote or contract roles, I am targeting **$110,000 to $130,000 base** or **$55 to $70 an hour**.

  [Recruiter Name], what is the budgeted compensation band or rate approved by your client for this position?"

*(Strategy: Always bounce the question back to them so they state the client's approved budget first).*

---

### Minute 4:00 to 5:00: Taking Control & Closing Next Steps
**Goal:** Obtain the written job description and secure a direct technical panel interview.

* **Dedeepya:**  
  "That sounds like an excellent match with my background in Databricks lakehouse migrations and PySpark pipeline engineering.

  Could you please email me the official Job Description, the client name, and the role requisition ID to **majetydedeepya0@gmail.com**?

  Once I review the specific requirements, I will confirm my alignment via email and we can coordinate presenting my profile to the hiring manager."

* **Recruiter:** "Absolutely, I will send that over today."
* **Dedeepya:**  
  "Thank you so much for your time today, [Recruiter Name]! I look forward to receiving your email and moving forward with the team. Have a great rest of your day!"

## 🛑 3 "Don't Do This" Rules on the Call

1. **Do NOT sound like an entry-level developer:**  
   Never say "I just do what senior developers assign me" or "I am looking for junior roles."  
   Say: *"I have 3+ years of enterprise data engineering experience at Cognizant, owning production ETL modernization to Azure Databricks Delta Lake."*

2. **Do NOT list tools without business impact:**  
   Never just recite "I know Python, Spark, and ADF."  
   Always ground skills in concrete outcomes: *"Reduced pipeline runtimes by 40% and cut cloud compute costs by ~30% while preserving 100% data parity."*

3. **Do NOT commit to verbal representation or lowball rates on the phone:**  
   If an agency recruiter asks "Can I submit your resume at ₹10 LPA / $40/hr right now?",  
   Say: *"Please send the written JD and approved client rate card to my email first. I will review the scope and confirm promptly."*
