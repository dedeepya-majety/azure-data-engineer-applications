## 4. Remote Interview Strategy for Indian GCCs & Product Companies

In the Indian tech market (specifically Hyderabad and Chennai), **virtually all initial and technical interview rounds are conducted remotely**:
1. **Virtual Infrastructure:** Recruiter screenings and technical coding/architecture interviews are hosted on **Microsoft Teams** or **Zoom**, with coding shared via HackerRank / CoderPad.
2. **Onsite Requirement (Post-Selection Only):** Only a small fraction of firms require an in-person final round; most GCCs (BMS, Novartis, Providence, Sanofi, Amazon, Uber) execute **100% remote hiring with virtual document verification**.
3. **Joining Advantage:** Being on active notice period with **30-day availability** accelerates scheduling: recruiters prioritize immediate joiners to fill quarterly headcount quotas.
